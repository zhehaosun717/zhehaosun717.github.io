01 — About

About Me

I’m a game designer and MA student in Interdisciplinary Graduate Studies (Digital Arts & Humanities) at UBC Okanagan, supervised by Dr. Megan Smith. My research interests are game mechanics and player experience, together with procedural content generation and AI-assisted game design.

I hold a BFA in Interactive Design & Game Development from SCAD, and I build when research needs a playable form: Unreal, Unity, WebGL, and shaders.

UBC Okanagan · Work Study · 2026 — Present

Beaver World

3D educational game, team project. My role: Technical Artist / Developer. Shown: the main menu over a live 3D scene.

Unity HDRP Procedural Generation Optimization

Built a procedural vegetation generator that fills five 4 km² scenes with trees, shrubs and grass from per-scene ecological rules. Reduced triangles per frame from 255 M to about 40 M and halved texture memory (3.5 GB to 1.7 GB). Created the wildfire visuals and post-fire regrowth, and designed the main menu with its live 3D background scene.
